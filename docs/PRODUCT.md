# Tribea Website: Product

## What It Is

The Tribea website presents the agency to four audiences, in this order: candidates, parties and committees; cantonal and communal administrations; journalists; and future team members and partners. Its single message is that Tribea brings the latest communication technology to Swiss politics and uses it with care, privacy-first and within Swiss law.

## Primary Flow

A candidate, party or committee lands on the home page in their language, reads what Tribea does and how it differs, opens the service that matches their situation (Profile, Campaign or Trust), and books a first conversation. Every page ends on that next step.

## Structure

Home, Offer (with Profile, Campaign and Trust one level down), Public sector, How we work (with the technology policy and the plain-language privacy page), Analysis, About and Contact, plus the legal notice, privacy policy and accessibility statement. No page is more than two clicks from the home page, and every page has its own address in each language.
